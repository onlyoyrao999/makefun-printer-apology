A one-line apology from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it — written for make.fun's "The Printer Apology" bounty. The printer is sorry it ate your report, but in its defence, it was fed staples, cheap paper and false hope, and it is still out of cyan.

## Run it
